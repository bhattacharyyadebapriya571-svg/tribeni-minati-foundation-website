# CSR & Grant Writing Skill

## Purpose
Formulates institutional-grade CSR funding proposals and grant applications for Tribeni Minati Foundation matching Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013.

## Frameworks & Pillars
1. **Pillar Mapping (M-I-N-A-T-I)**:
   - **M (Minorities)**: Skill development, micro-entrepreneurship, community cohesion (Schedule VII Item ii).
   - **I (Illiterate)**: Minati Free Remedial Coaching Centers, digital literacy, midday nutrition (Schedule VII Item ii).
   - **N (Needy)**: Winter infant bedding kits, emergency flood/cyclone relief, food security (Schedule VII Item i).
   - **A (Abused)**: Swabhiman vocational tailoring, women dignity, legal aid (Schedule VII Item iii).
   - **T (Tribal)**: Tribal mobile medical vans, potable drinking water borewells (Schedule VII Item i & x).
   - **I (Indians)**: Voluntary blood donation, disaster resilience (Schedule VII Item xii).

2. **Standard Proposal Structure**:
   - Executive Summary with verified IDs (DARPAN `WB/2026/0939703`, Reg `SO212276`, 80G Approval).
   - Problem Statement & Baseline Ground Survey data in Hooghly District.
   - Proposed Intervention, Logframe Matrix, and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).
   - Detailed Budget Breakdown with unit economics and milestone schedule.
   - Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) framework with quarterly geo-tagged photographic evidence.
