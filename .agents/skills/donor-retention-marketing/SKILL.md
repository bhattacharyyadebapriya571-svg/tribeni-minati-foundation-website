# Donor Retention & Marketing Skill

## Purpose
Drives high-integrity donor acquisition and lifetime donor loyalty for Tribeni Minati Foundation, maximizing the impact of the primary slogan `"...your smile, our reward..."` and action motto `"...Lets go.. Do something!!"`.

## Core Conversion Strategies
1. **80G Tax Exemption Clarity**:
   - Clearly communicate 50% deduction under Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act.
   - Offer dynamic tax relief calculation for Old vs New tax regimes.
   - Provide instant downloadable digitally-signed receipt with 80G approval number and donor PAN.

2. **Donor Journey Automation**:
   - **T+0**: Instant WhatsApp/Email thank-you with transaction receipt and real photo of the impacted center.
   - **T+30 Days**: Impact photo-update of the child sponsored or village health camp conducted.
   - **T+90 Days**: Quarterly ground progress newsletter with annual audit transparency ledger.
   - **Tax Season (Feb-March)**: Year-end consolidated 80G tax deduction pack for CA filing.

3. **Trust & Payment Friction Reduction**:
   - Prominent Central Bank of India official account display (A/C: `5894594000`, IFSC: `CBIN0283860`).
   - One-click copy for UPI ID `tribeniminati@cbin` and direct QR code payment.
