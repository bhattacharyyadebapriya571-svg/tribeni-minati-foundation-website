# Field Impact Storytelling Skill

## Purpose
Crafts evocative, respectful, and authentic human-centered narratives for Tribeni Minati Foundation's 26 real field photographs and on-ground campaigns across West Bengal.

## Narrative Guidelines
1. **Dignity-First Storytelling**:
   - Focus on agency, resilience, and transformation rather than helpless victimhood.
   - Accurately attribute real locations: Tribeni Kanthaltala, Mogra, Dhaniakhali Radhanagar, and Hooghly rural hamlets.

2. **Core Impact Arcs**:
   - **Minati Pathshala (Illiterate Pillar)**: First-generation school-goers gaining reading fluency and arithmetic mastery under the village banyan tree.
   - **Winter Survival Kits (Needy Pillar)**: Rural infants and elderly mothers receiving insulated blankets during biting winter temperatures.
   - **Swabhiman Tailoring (Abused Pillar)**: Women completing handloom and tailoring certification to gain financial independence.
   - **Mobile Tribal Clinics (Tribal Pillar)**: Preventive health screenings, eye tests, and clean water distribution in marginalized settlements.

3. **Multi-Channel Delivery**:
   - Web Story Cards, 3D interactive feeds, press releases, and WhatsApp dispatches.
