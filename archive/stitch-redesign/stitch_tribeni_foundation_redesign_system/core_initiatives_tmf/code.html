Trajectory of Impact

Milestones achieved through institutional partnerships and community trust.

Foundation Established

Initiated local outreach programs in West Bengal, registering as a statutory trust with a focus on systemic rural aid.

2022

First Medical Drive

Partnered with district medical officers to execute the first 1,000-patient pediatric camp in remote Sundarbans.

Remedial Centers Launch

Opened 3 permanent education centers serving 500+ daily students with smart-board integration and nutritional support.
